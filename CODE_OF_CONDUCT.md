# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (version 2.1), summarised here.

**Our pledge.** We want everyone to be able to take part, whatever their experience, background, identity or opinions, without harassment.

**Expected**: be respectful and patient; give and take criticism of the work, not of the person; say it when you do not know; stay on the subject of the project in its issues and pull requests.

**Not acceptable**: harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing other people's private information, threats, sexualised language or attention, and anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

**Enforcement.** The maintainer ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)) may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block a person from the project's repository. Report a problem privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting of this repository (Security → Report a vulnerability: choose "other" in the text), or in a first message to the maintainer that holds no details; reports are handled in confidence and answered within 7 days.

This applies in the project's spaces (the repository, its issues and pull requests) and when someone represents the project in public.
